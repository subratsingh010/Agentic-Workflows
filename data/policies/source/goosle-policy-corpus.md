# Goosle Policy Corpus

## Goosle Access Request Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-access-policy -->

### access-001
<!-- chunk_id: access-001 -->
System access must follow least privilege, role-based approval, and periodic review. Access requests require business justification and manager or system owner approval.

### access-002
<!-- chunk_id: access-002 -->
Privileged access to production systems may require additional security approval, time limits, break-glass logging, and post-use review.

### access-003
<!-- chunk_id: access-003 -->
The employee support agent may explain access policy but cannot grant, revoke, or approve access.

## Goosle Responsible AI Usage Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-ai-policy -->

### ai-usage-001
<!-- chunk_id: ai-usage-001 -->
Employees may use approved AI tools for drafting, summarization, coding assistance, research, and workflow automation when data classification rules are followed.

### ai-usage-002
<!-- chunk_id: ai-usage-002 -->
Employees must not enter secrets, customer personal data, confidential financials, merger information, or unreleased product plans into unapproved AI tools.

### ai-usage-003
<!-- chunk_id: ai-usage-003 -->
AI-generated content should be reviewed by the employee before use. Employees remain accountable for accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance.

## Goosle Code of Conduct
<!-- document_id: goosle-code-of-conduct -->

### conduct-001
<!-- chunk_id: conduct-001 -->
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, candidates, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, and abusive behavior are prohibited.

### conduct-002
<!-- chunk_id: conduct-002 -->
Concerns may be reported to a manager, HR, Legal, Employee Relations, or the Ethics hotline. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.

### conduct-003
<!-- chunk_id: conduct-003 -->
Managers must escalate safety concerns, harassment reports, bribery concerns, conflicts of interest, and suspected legal violations to the appropriate team.

## Goosle Device and Access Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-device-policy -->

### devices-001
<!-- chunk_id: devices-001 -->
Goosle-issued laptops must use disk encryption, endpoint protection, automatic screen lock, and managed operating system updates.

### devices-002
<!-- chunk_id: devices-002 -->
Local administrator rights require a business justification, manager approval, and may be time-limited. Admin rights must not be used to disable security tooling.

### devices-003
<!-- chunk_id: devices-003 -->

## Goosle Travel and Expense Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-expense-policy -->

### expenses-001
<!-- chunk_id: expenses-001 -->
Goosle reimburses reasonable business expenses that are necessary, documented, and approved. Expense claims should include a receipt, business purpose, cost center, and approver.

### expenses-002
<!-- chunk_id: expenses-002 -->
Expense claims should be submitted within thirty days of the transaction. Late claims may require manager and finance review.

### expenses-003
<!-- chunk_id: expenses-003 -->
Employees should book standard economy airfare for flights under six hours and may book premium economy for longer business flights when approved.

### expenses-004
<!-- chunk_id: expenses-004 -->
Personal travel upgrades, fines, lost personal items, family travel, and unapproved entertainment are not reimbursable unless pre-approved by HR or Finance.

Employees may request temporary remote work for focused work, caregiving, health needs, severe weather, or travel disruption when business needs allow.

### incident-reporting-001

## Goosle Jira Time Logging Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-jira-time-policy -->

### jira-time-logging-001
<!-- chunk_id: jira-time-logging-001 -->
Engineering, product, design, and support employees are expected to log work time in Jira against the relevant issue by the end of each working day.

### jira-time-logging-002
<!-- chunk_id: jira-time-logging-002 -->
Jira time logs should use the actual work date, relevant issue key, concise work description, and realistic hours. Placeholder logs should be corrected before sprint close.

### jira-time-logging-003
<!-- chunk_id: jira-time-logging-003 -->
Managers may review Jira time logs for project accounting, sprint planning, capitalization reporting, and customer support commitments, but logs do not automatically create payroll records.

### jira-time-logging-004
<!-- chunk_id: jira-time-logging-004 -->
The Goosle employee support agent can look up Jira time logs but cannot create Jira tickets, change issue status, edit assignees, or modify sprint scope.

## Goosle Leave Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-leave-policy -->

### leave-001
<!-- chunk_id: leave-001 -->
Goosle employees may request vacation, sick, personal, parental, bereavement, and unpaid leave. Vacation leave should be requested before the start date and requires manager approval.

### leave-002
<!-- chunk_id: leave-002 -->
Vacation leave requests should include start date, end date, leave type, and optional reason. Requests longer than ten business days may require director review.

### leave-003
<!-- chunk_id: leave-003 -->
Sick leave may be submitted after the start date when the employee is unexpectedly ill. A medical note may be requested only where permitted by local law and absence exceeds five consecutive working days.

### leave-004
<!-- chunk_id: leave-004 -->
Bereavement leave may be used after the death of an immediate family member or household member. Employees should notify their manager as soon as practical.

### leave-005
<!-- chunk_id: leave-005 -->
Personal leave may be used for urgent personal matters, civic obligations, or short-term family needs when vacation or sick leave is not appropriate.

## Goosle Onboarding Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-onboarding-policy -->

### onboarding-001
<!-- chunk_id: onboarding-001 -->
New hires should complete required onboarding tasks in the first ten business days, including security training, benefits enrollment, device setup, code of conduct attestation, and role-specific orientation.

### onboarding-002
<!-- chunk_id: onboarding-002 -->
Managers should provide a thirty-day onboarding plan with team introductions, first assignments, required systems, success measures, and check-in cadence.

### onboarding-003
<!-- chunk_id: onboarding-003 -->
New hires should not receive production access until mandatory security training, access approval, and role-based authorization are complete.

<!-- document_id: goosle-parental-leave -->

### parental-leave-001

## Goosle Records Retention Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-records-policy -->

### records-001

Business records must be retained according to the records retention schedule and legal hold notices. Employees must not delete records subject to legal hold.

### records-002
<!-- chunk_id: records-002 -->
Drafts, working notes, and duplicate files should be cleaned up when no longer needed unless they are part of an active legal hold or business record.

### records-003
<!-- chunk_id: records-003 -->

## Goosle Information Security Policy
<!-- document_id: goosle-security-policy -->

### security-001
<!-- chunk_id: security-001 -->
Employees must protect confidential information, customer data, credentials, and source code. Company data may only be stored in approved systems.

### security-002
<!-- chunk_id: security-002 -->
Employees must not paste secrets, customer data, unpublished financial information, or confidential source code into unapproved tools.

### security-003
<!-- chunk_id: security-003 -->
Goosle requires multi-factor authentication for workforce systems, source repositories, cloud consoles, and production support tools.

### security-004
<!-- chunk_id: security-004 -->
Lost devices, suspected phishing, exposed credentials, suspicious login prompts, and accidental data sharing must be reported to Security immediately.

## Goosle Supported Agent Actions
<!-- document_id: goosle-supported-agent-actions -->

### agent-scope-001
<!-- chunk_id: agent-scope-001 -->
The Goosle employee support agent can answer policy questions, look up Jira time logs, and submit leave applications with confirmation.

### agent-scope-002
<!-- chunk_id: agent-scope-002 -->
The agent cannot create Jira tickets, approve expenses, modify payroll, reset passwords, approve leave, change HR records, or decide employee permissions.

### agent-scope-003
<!-- chunk_id: agent-scope-003 -->
Protected actions require backend authorization, typed validation, idempotency checks, confirmation when needed, and audit logging before execution.
